How to pay $0 when you're sick

  • Book a same-day sick visit with a primary care doctor at an ACPNY office or H+H Gotham Health clinic. That visit is $0, and labs and X-rays there are $0 too.
  • ACPNY: 646-680-4227. H+H: 844-692-4692.
  • Don't book it as "urgent care": that's $25 at H+H and $50 at ACPNY.
  • Need a work or school note? Ask before you leave. A Teladoc video visit is $10 and can usually give one too.

💊 Prescription drugs

Most drugs go through OptumRx, a separate card from DC 37. Customer service 1-800-207-1561 (24/7), optumrx.com.

Tier30-day retail90-day retail90-day mail

  • Which card: OptumRx for most drugs. Your NYCE PPO card covers diabetes meds, low-dose statins, PrEP, birth control, and vaccines (often $0). The Express Scripts/PICA card covers self-injectables and cancer drugs.
  • Save money: use mail order for daily meds. 90 days costs 2 copays instead of 3. Allow 14 days for delivery.
  • If you choose a brand drug that has a generic, you pay the price difference plus the copay.
  • Step therapy: for blood pressure, diabetes, depression, asthma and allergy meds, you must try the generic "Step One" drug first.
  • Specialty drugs: need prior approval and go through Optum Specialty Pharmacy, 1-855-427-4682.
Problem at the pharmacy? If they say "not covered" or want more than $45.50 for 30 days:
  1. Ask them to run it through each card (OptumRx, NYCE PPO, PICA).
  2. Still stuck? Call 212-815-1234 and say you're at the pharmacy.
  • Prior authorization questions: 212-815-1608
  • Lost card: 212-815-1234
  • Paid out of pocket? Ask the Plan office for a Prescription Drug Reimbursement form and send it with your receipt. You get back the contracted rate minus your copay.

👓 Vision (optical)

Every 24 months, pick one of these:

  1. Voucher: covers an exam, frames and lenses at a participating optical store. See the Vision tab for the full list.
  2. Reimbursement: go anywhere and get back up to $250.
  3. Both: the voucher's value is subtracted from the $250. You must wait 60 days after the voucher before claiming reimbursement.

Step-by-step instructions are in the Vision tab.

🛌 Disability (short-term)
  • If you can't work because of illness or injury that's not work-related: $300/week full-time or $200/week part-time, up to 26 weeks.
  • Deadline: submit within 30 days of your last day of work.
  • Fill out every section: your part, your doctor's part with treatment dates, and your timekeeper's contact details. Missing pieces delay payment.
  • Fax 212-298-9886 or call 212-815-1234. The form is in the Forms tab.
⚖️ Free legal help (MELS)

  • Full-time members also get: buying or selling a home, custody, orders of protection, adoption, name changes.

🔪 Second opinion before surgery

If a surgeon recommends elective surgery, you can get a second opinion from a doctor you choose, and the plan pays that visit's copay. You don't have to follow either opinion. Questions: 212-815-1234.

🕊️ Death & accident benefits
  • Death benefit to your beneficiary: $10,000 full-time, $6,000 part-time.
  • Accidental death: an extra $10,000 full-time or $6,000 part-time.
  • Loss of a limb or sight: $5,000 per loss full-time, up to $10,000.
  • A family member must file within 1 year, with a death certificate. Fax 212-298-9885.
  • Your spouse or dependents can keep health benefits 12 months after your death, plus up to 24 more under COBRA.
  • Action item: make sure your beneficiary form is current (Forms tab).

How to use your vision benefit

Option A: Voucher (easiest, little or no money up front)

  1. Call 212-815-1234 to confirm you're eligible and request a voucher.
  2. Pick a store from the list below. Call it to confirm it still takes DC 37 vouchers, since the list changes monthly.
  3. Bring the voucher and your ID. It covers an exam, frames and lenses from the store's voucher selection. Upgrades cost extra.

Option B: Reimbursement (any store or eye doctor, up to $250 every 24 months)

  1. Pay for glasses, contacts, an exam, or prescription sunglasses.
  2. Have the eye doctor or store fill out the Optical Reimbursement Form (Forms tab). Claims without a completed form aren't processed.
  3. Attach itemized receipts and proof of payment.
  4. Mail to: DC 37 H&S Plan Optical Unit, 125 Barclay St, New York, NY 10007. You can also email it to the Optical Unit address listed on dc37.net.

Using both: the voucher's value comes off your $250, and you must wait 60 days after the voucher to file for reimbursement.
