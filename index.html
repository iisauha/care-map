See a doctor for $0

Book a same-day sick visit with a primary care doctor at ACPNY or NYC Health + Hospitals. You pay nothing, and labs and X-rays are free too.

  1. Call one of these in the morning AdvantageCare (ACPNY) 646-680-4227 NYC Health + Hospitals 844-692-4692
  2. Say this
    "I'm sick and need a same-day sick visit with a primary care doctor."

    Don't ask for "urgent care." That's billed differently and costs $25 to $50.

Getting a doctor's note

Any doctor who sees you can write one. The cheapest way:

  1. Book a $0 sick visitat ACPNY or NYC Health + Hospitals. How to book
  2. Ask for the note at the end of the visit
    "Can I get a note for work covering today through [date]?"
  3. Check it before you leaveIt should have the dates you were out and the doctor's name and signature. Ask if your job needs anything specific, like a return-to-work date.

Too sick to go in?

A $10 Teladoc video visit can usually give you a note too. Ask during the call.

Prescriptions

Most prescriptions go through your OptumRx card from DC 37, not your medical card.

What you pay

Generic90 days by mail: $20$10
Preferred brand90 days by mail: $40$20
Non-preferred brand90 days by mail: $91$45.50

Prices are for a 30-day supply at a pharmacy. For daily meds, mail order saves you a copay every 3 months.

Which card to show

OptumRxMost medications
NYCE PPO cardDiabetes meds, low-dose statins, birth control, PrEP, vaccines
Express Scripts (PICA)Self-injectables and cancer drugs

Problem at the pharmacy?

If they say it isn't covered, or want more than $45.50 for 30 days:

  1. Ask them to try each cardOptumRx, NYCE PPO, and PICA.
  2. Still stuck? Call from the pharmacyDC 37 Prescription Unit 212-815-1234

    Tell them you're at the pharmacy now.

More details
  • If you pick a brand when a generic exists, you pay the price difference plus the copay.
  • For blood pressure, diabetes, depression, asthma and allergy meds, you have to try the generic first ("step therapy").
  • Specialty drugs need approval first and go through Optum Specialty Pharmacy, 1-855-427-4682.
  • Prior authorization questions: 212-815-1608.
  • OptumRx customer service (24/7): 1-800-207-1561.
  • Paid full price? Call 212-815-1234 for a reimbursement form and send it with your receipt.

Glasses and eye exams

Every 2 years, DC 37 covers glasses or contacts one of two ways.

Best if you want to pay little or nothing. It covers an exam, frames and lenses from the store's voucher selection.

  1. Request a voucherDC 37 Health & Security 212-815-1234
  2. Pick a store below and call itMake sure it still takes DC 37 vouchers. The list changes monthly.
  3. Bring the voucher and your IDUpgrades like designer frames or special coatings cost extra.

Best if you want a specific store or eye doctor. You pay first, then get back up to $250.

  1. Buy your glasses, contacts or exam anywhere
  2. Have the store fill out the reimbursement formClaims without it aren't processed. Open the form
  3. Attach itemized receipts and proof of payment
  4. Mail itDC 37 H&S Plan Optical Unit, 125 Barclay St, New York, NY 10007.

Used a voucher already? Its value comes off the $250, and you have to wait 60 days.
