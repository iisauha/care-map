## Design Context

### Users
NYC city workers on the NYCE PPO health plan who are also DC 37 union members. They open this on an iPhone (often as a Home Screen PWA), usually when they're sick, hurt, or trying to figure out where care is cheap *right now* — not a leisurely browse. Health-plan content (copays, benefit rules) skews anxious/confused; the interface should lower that anxiety, not add to it.

### Brand Personality
From the creator's own HANDOFF.md: "simple, intuitive, plain language, question-based (I'm sick, Walk-in, Emergency). No emojis. Less is more. Clean, native-iOS feel; must not look like generic AI output." No em dashes in copy. Trustworthy and calm over flashy — this is utility software for a moment of mild stress, not a marketing site.

### Aesthetic Direction
Renamed Health Hub (Nov 2026 redesign, mockups in Figma). Native-iOS feel: home is one screen with no scrolling. It asks "What do you need?" with icon chips, shows the answer on the map, then a swipeable carousel of nearby places. Bottom sheets never scroll. Light and dark both supported (Light/Dark/Auto setting).

Logo: a hub with four rounded spokes that reads as a soft medical plus, cream on deep pine with an apricot center. App icons are generated from the same drawing (icons/icon.svg).

Color: warm oat neutrals instead of gray, no pure white or pure black anywhere large, one pine accent.
- Light: page #f4f1ea, raised surfaces #fbfaf6, fills #ebe7dd, ink #1c2b26, accent pine #1f5c4a.
- Dark: base #121715, raised #1b221f, fills #252e2a, ink #ece8df, accent mint #8fd3b4.
- Category tones (free, walk-in, $100, ER, dentist, glasses) stay distinct, about 50% L in light and lighter and desaturated in dark. Every text accent clears 4.5:1 on its surface.
- Map: OSM tiles toned with a per-theme CSS filter (CARTO tiles need an API key, which this public repo avoids).

Type: Fraunces (variable, optical sizes) for titles, place names and prices; Figtree for everything else. Both SIL OFL and self-hosted in vendor/fonts. Figtree has a plain zero, so "$0" reads correctly and the old digit fallback is gone.

Icons: one set on a 24px grid with a 1.8 stroke and round ends (assets/app.js `I`). Tinted squircles carry a price or an icon in its category's color.

### Design Principles
1. Plain, question-based language over clinical jargon ("I'm sick" not "Request outpatient consultation").
2. Calm and trustworthy over bold or playful — this is health-plan utility software, not a product with room for daring.
3. One native-feeling bottom sheet pattern, used consistently everywhere (home, filters, place detail).
4. Native-iOS motion and density over web-app conventions (no modals, no generic cards-with-shadow grids).
5. Must not read as AI-generated: no gradient text, no glassmorphism, no identical icon-card grids, no generic rounded-shadow cards.
