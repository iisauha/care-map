## Design Context

### Users
NYC city workers on the NYCE PPO health plan who are also DC 37 union members. They open this on an iPhone (often as a Home Screen PWA), usually when they're sick, hurt, or trying to figure out where care is cheap *right now* — not a leisurely browse. Health-plan content (copays, benefit rules) skews anxious/confused; the interface should lower that anxiety, not add to it.

### Brand Personality
From the creator's own HANDOFF.md: "simple, intuitive, plain language, question-based (I'm sick, Walk-in, Emergency). No emojis. Less is more. Clean, native-iOS feel; must not look like generic AI output." No em dashes in copy. Trustworthy and calm over flashy — this is utility software for a moment of mild stress, not a marketing site.

### Aesthetic Direction
Native-iOS feel (Apple Maps-style full-screen map + bottom sheet is the explicit reference). Light and dark both supported via `prefers-color-scheme` (used any time of day/night, no single context to optimize for). Anti-reference: generic AI-generated interface look. Currently uses a single flat green accent (#17694a light / #3fae7f dark) against neutral off-white/near-black surfaces, system font stack.

### Design Principles
1. Plain, question-based language over clinical jargon ("I'm sick" not "Request outpatient consultation").
2. Calm and trustworthy over bold or playful — this is health-plan utility software, not a product with room for daring.
3. One native-feeling bottom sheet pattern, used consistently everywhere (home, filters, place detail).
4. Native-iOS motion and density over web-app conventions (no modals, no generic cards-with-shadow grids).
5. Must not read as AI-generated: no gradient text, no glassmorphism, no identical icon-card grids, no generic rounded-shadow cards.
